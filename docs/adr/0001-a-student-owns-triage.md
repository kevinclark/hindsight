# 1. A student owns triage at an event

Status: accepted, 2026-10-23

## Context

At an event someone keeps the list of open problems and decides what gets fixed before the next match. Hindsight's
wording, defaults and priority view are written for whoever that is. The team's motivation rule (David Yeager, *10 to
25*: high standards with visible support) says adults don't quietly do the students' work.

## Decision

A student lead (pit or software) owns triage. Mentors see the same board and coach. A mentor can take it over in
special circumstances, and the board shows who changed what, so a takeover is visible and hands back cleanly.

## Consequences

Every triage screen must be usable by a student under time pressure, and nothing in it assumes a mentor's context.
